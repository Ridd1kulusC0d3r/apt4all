Português: COMECE-AQUI.md

One thing at a time. Each step takes about 5 minutes and ends with something in hand. Tick it and move on.

Step 1 · Open the Atlas

Done when: you see the hex map with China in bright amber.

Step 2 · Read a country

Done when: the right panel shows the country dossier (actors, motivations, techniques, sectors).

Step 3 · Read an actor

Done when: you can name three other names for the same group. (Hint: they are at the top of the card.)

Step 4 · Build your organization's threat profile

Done when: navigator.json is in your downloads folder.

Step 5 · See the techniques in the Navigator

Done when: you see techniques painted amber. The brightest ones appear most often across the actors in your profile: start your defense there.

Finished? Pick one of these for tomorrow:

  • Study with a method: Learning path, module 1
  • Find a tool or source: Resources, filter ★ start here
  • Take the profile into threat modeling: TCE bridge
